In this article, you will learn how to integrate Superflow with Asana.

Access the Superflow Dashboard by going to [app.usesuperflow.com](app.usesuperflow.com). Alternatively, when you are in Review Mode, you can use the method below:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the Superflow Toolbar and click the More Options icon">
    <Frame>
    ![Find the Superflow Toolbar and click the More Options icon](/images/dashboard/how-to-access-project-settings/how-to-access-project-settings/INT1FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="A sub-menu will expand, click the View Project option to select">
  </Step>
  <Step title="A new tab will direct you to the Project Dashboard page">
    <Note>
    By default, you will be directed to the Dashboard for Comment Status screen when accessing the **Project Dashboard**.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the INTEGRATIONS module to access more integration options">
    <Frame>
    ![Click the INTEGRATIONS module to access more integration options](/images/integrations/how-to-integrate-with-asana/AS1FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select ASANA to access Asana’s integration page and click Connect to start the integration">
  </Step>
  <Step title="For this example, we will use a Gmail account">
    Click the **Continue with Google** button.

    <Note>
    If you are using non-Google email, use the **Email address** field to continue
    </Note>

    <Frame>
    ![For this example, we will use a Gmail account](/images/integrations/how-to-integrate-with-asana/AS2FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="You will be directed to the Sign in with Google page…">
    You will be directed to the **Sign in with Google** page, find and select the email that you want to use.

    <Frame>
    ![You will be directed to the Sign in with Google page…](/images/integrations/how-to-integrate-with-asana/AS3FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="You will be directed to the Grant Permission page…">
    You will be directed to the **Grant Permission** page, click **Allow** to allow Superflow to access your Asana account.

    <Frame>
    ![You will be directed to the Grant Permission page…](/images/integrations/how-to-integrate-with-asana/AS4FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
