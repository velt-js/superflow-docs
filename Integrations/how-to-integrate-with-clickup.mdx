In this article, you will learn how to integrate Superflow with ClickUp.

Access the Superflow Dashboard by going to [app.usesuperflow.com](app.usesuperflow.com). Alternatively, when you are in Review Mode, you can use the method below:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the Superflow Toolbar and click the More Options icon">
    <Frame>
    ![Find the Superflow Toolbar and click the More Options icon](/images/dashboard/how-to-access-project-settings/how-to-access-project-settings/INT1FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="A sub-menu will expand, click the View Project option to select">
  </Step>
  <Step title="A new tab will direct you to the Project Dashboard page">
    <Note>
    By default, you will be directed to the Dashboard for Comment Status screen when accessing the **Project Dashboard**.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the INTEGRATIONS module to access more integration options">
    <Frame>
    ![Click the INTEGRATIONS module to access more integration options](/images/integrations/how-to-integrate-with-clickup/CU1FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select CLICKUP to access ClickUp’s integration page and click Connect to start the integration">
  </Step>
  <Step title="For this example, we will use a Gmail account">
    Click the **Sign in with Google** button.

    <Note>
    If you are using non-Google email, use the **Email** and **Password** fields to continue
    </Note>

    <Frame>
    ![For this example, we will use a Gmail account](/images/integrations/how-to-integrate-with-clickup/CU2FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="You will be directed to the Sign in with Google page…">
    You will be directed to the **Sign in with Google** page, find and select the email that you want to use.

    <Frame>
    ![You will be directed to the Sign in with Google page…](/images/integrations/how-to-integrate-with-clickup/CU3FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="You will be directed to the Confirmation page…">
    You will be directed to the **Confirmation** page, click **Confirm** to allow Superflow to access your ClickUp account.

    <Frame>
    ![You will be directed to the Confirmation page…](/images/integrations/how-to-integrate-with-clickup/CU4FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="You will be directed to the Connect ClickUp page…">
    You will be directed to the **Connect ClickUp** page, click the **Connect Workspace** button to complete the integration.

    <Frame>
    ![You will be directed to the Connect ClickUp page…](/images/integrations/how-to-integrate-with-clickup/CU5FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
