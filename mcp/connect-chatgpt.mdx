---
title: "Connect ChatGPT"
description: "Add the Superflow MCP connector to ChatGPT as a custom connector."
icon: "comments"
---

Custom connectors are available to ChatGPT users on Pro, Plus, Business, Enterprise and Edu plans. On managed workspaces, an admin must allow custom connectors.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings, then Connectors">
    If your workspace requires it for custom MCP connectors, enable **Developer mode** first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a custom connector">
    Use the server URL:

    ```
    https://mcp.usesuperflow.ai/mcp
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and approve">
    ChatGPT sends you to Superflow's consent screen. Sign in, pick the workspace to connect, review the [permissions](/mcp/overview#permissions), and click **Approve**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Then ask things like: "How many open comment threads does my team have this week?" or "Create a Superflow project for staging.acme.com and give me the install snippet."
