In this article, you will learn how to add a priority. There are three (3) levels of priorities starting at P0, P1, and P2. There are a couple of ways to add priorities, either by creating a comment and adding a priority or adding priority to an existing comment. In this example, we will use the latter.

<Steps>
  <Step title="On the screen, find and click the Comment that you want to update">
    <Frame>
    ![On the screen, find and click the Comment that you want to update](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-add-a-priority/DC1FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="The Comment Box will appear, click the Flag icon beside the status">
    <Frame>
    ![The Comment Box will appear, click the Flag icon beside the status](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-add-a-priority/AP2FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="The Priority sub-menu will expand">
    Click the appropriate priority to select. After selecting the priority, the system will automatically apply the changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>
