In this article, you will learn how to delete existing comments.

<Steps>
  <Step title="On the screen, find and click the Comment you want to delete">
    <Frame>
    ![On the screen, find and click the Comment you want to delete](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-delete-comments/DC1FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="The Comment Box will appear, click the Menu (...) to enable more options">
    <Frame>
    ![The Comment Box will appear, click the Menu (...) to enable more options](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-delete-comments/DC2FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Delete Thread">
    A **Confirmation** dialog box will appear. Click **Delete** to continue deleting the comment.

    <Frame>
    ![Click Delete Thread](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-delete-comments/DC3FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
