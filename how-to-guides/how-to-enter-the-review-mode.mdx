---
title: "How to Enter the Review Mode"
description: "Add ?review=true to any page's URL to bring up the Superflow Toolbar."
icon: "eye"
---

Review mode is what brings up the Superflow Toolbar on a site where Superflow is installed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the page you want to review">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add ?review=true to the end of the URL and press Enter">
    For example: `mywebsite.com/?review=true`. If the URL already has a `?` in it, append `&review=true` instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The toolbar appears in the bottom corner">
    <Frame>
    ![A site in review mode with the Superflow toolbar in the bottom right](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-enter-the-review-mode/01-review-mode.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Review mode sticks as you navigate: once entered, the toolbar stays on every page of the site until you [exit review mode](/how-to-guides/how-to-exit-the-review-mode). Sharing a link with `?review=true` included is the easiest way to invite someone to comment.
</Note>
