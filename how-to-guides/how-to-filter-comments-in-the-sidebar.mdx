title: "How to Filter Comments in the Sidebar"
description: "Learn how to use the Filter option under the Comments Sidebar feature."
icon: "filter"
---

In this article, you will learn how to use the Filter option under the Comments Sidebar feature. This option lets you filter comments based on pages, people, category, priority, and group.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the Superflow Toolbar and click the Open Comments icon">
    <Frame>
    ![Find the Superflow Toolbar and click the Open Comments icon](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-open-the-comments-sidebar/OCS1FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="The Comments Sidebar opens, click the Filter icon to expand the filter options">
    <Frame>
    ![The Comments Sidebar opens, click the Filter icon to expand the filter options](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-filter-comments-in-the-sidebar/CFS1FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="The Filters section will appear…">
    The **Filters** section will appear, you can filter the comment by selecting the appropriate checkbox:

    * Pages
    * People
    * Category
    * Priority
    * Group By

    <Frame>
    ![The Filters section will appear…](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-filter-comments-in-the-sidebar/CFS2FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
