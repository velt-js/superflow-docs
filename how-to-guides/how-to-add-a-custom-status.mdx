## How to Add a Custom Status

In this article, you will learn how to create a custom status. Custom statuses allow you to add your own statuses, in addition to the default ones, so the review workflow in Superflow matches your team's internal workflow. Once created, a custom status can be applied to comments and will appear as a column in the Kanban board within the Superflow portal.

1. Go to [Advanced Features](https://app.usesuperflow.com/settings/advanced-features) in your Superflow portal settings.

2. Under **Comment Settings**, turn on **Enable custom status**.

3. Once enabled, click **Add New** in the custom status section. The **Add Status** dialog will appear.

<Frame>
![](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-add-a-custom-status/Add.png)
</Frame>

4. Enter a **Status Name**, pick a **Color**, and choose an **Icon** for your new status. The preview on the right shows how the status will look.

5. Click **Add Status** to save it.

6. Open any project and the new status will be available when changing a comment's status.

<Note>
You cannot edit a status once it has been created. To make changes, delete the status and create a new one. See [How to Delete a Custom Status](https://docs.usesuperflow.com/how-to-guides/how-to-delete-a-custom-status).
</Note>
