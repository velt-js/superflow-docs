---

In this article, you will learn how to record voice notes using the Superflow Toolbar.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the Superflow Toolbar and click the Comment icon">
    <Frame>
    ![Find the Superflow Toolbar and click the Comment icon](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-record-voice-notes/RVN1FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="After clicking the icon, click the Element you want to comment on">
    <Note>
    when you hover your cursor on an element, it will appear as a **Comment** icon which signifies that you selected the comment feature.
    </Note>

    <Frame>
    ![After clicking the icon, click the Element you want to comment on](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-record-voice-notes/RVN2FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="The Comment Box will appear, click the Microphone icon to select">
    <Frame>
    ![The Comment Box will appear, click the Microphone icon to select](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-record-voice-notes/RVN3FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="A Recording window will appear">
    Click the **Start Recording** button to begin recording the voice note.

    <Frame>
    ![A Recording window will appear](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-record-voice-notes/RVN4INAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="To complete your recording, click the Green Check Mark">
    This will stop the current recording.

    <Frame>
    ![To complete your recording, click the Green Check Mark](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-record-voice-notes/RVN5FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the Post button to save the voice note">
    <Frame>
    ![Click the Post button to save the voice note](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-record-voice-notes/RVN6FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
