description: "Learn how to leave video comments using the Superflow Toolbar."

---

In this article, you will learn how to leave video comments using the Superflow Toolbar.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the Superflow Toolbar and click the Comment icon">
    <Frame>
    ![Find the Superflow Toolbar and click the Comment icon](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-leave-video-comments/RVN1FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="After clicking the icon, click the Element you want to comment on">
    <Note>
    when you hover your cursor on an element, it will appear as a **Comment** icon which signifies that you selected the comment feature.
    </Note>

    <Frame>
    ![After clicking the icon, click the Element you want to comment on](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-leave-video-comments/RVN2FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="The Comment Box will appear, click the Camera icon to select it">
    <Frame>
    ![The Comment Box will appear, click the Camera icon to select it](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-leave-video-comments/LVC1FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="A Recording window will appear">
    Click the **Start Recording** button to begin recording the video comment.

    <Frame>
    ![A Recording window will appear](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-leave-video-comments/LVC2FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="To complete your recording, click the Red Stop Recording button to stop the current recording">
    <Frame>
    ![To complete your recording, click the Red Stop Recording button to stop the current recording](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-leave-video-comments/LVC3FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="You can add a comment or note in the Comment field…">
    You can add a comment or note in the **Comment** field and click the **Post** icon to save the video comment.

    <Frame>
    ![You can add a comment or note in the Comment field…](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-leave-video-comments/LVC4FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
