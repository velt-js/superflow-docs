---
title: "How to Record Screen and Add it to Comments"
description: "Learn how to record your screen and add it to comments."
icon: "display"
---

In this article, you will learn how to record your screen and add it to comments. This feature comes in handy when you need to record your screen without using another app and do more steps to record and add it to a comment.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the Superflow Toolbar and click the Comment icon">
    <Frame>
    ![Find the Superflow Toolbar and click the Comment icon](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-record-screen-and-add-it-to-comments/RVN1FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="After clicking the icon, click the Element you want to comment on,">
    <Note>
    when you hover your cursor on an element, it will appear as a **Comment** icon which signifies that you selected the comment feature.
    </Note>

    <Frame>
    ![After clicking the icon, click the Element you want to comment on,](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-record-screen-and-add-it-to-comments/RVN2FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="The Comment Box will appear, click the Screen Recorder icon to select it">
    <Frame>
    ![The Comment Box will appear, click the Screen Recorder icon to select it](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-record-screen-and-add-it-to-comments/RS1FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="A Recording window will appear">
    Click the **Start screen recording** button to begin screen recording.

    <Frame>
    ![A Recording window will appear](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-record-screen-and-add-it-to-comments/RS2FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="A new window will appear, find and click the window/screen that you want to record…">
    A new window will appear, find and click the window/screen that you want to record and click **Share**.

    <Frame>
    ![A new window will appear, find and click the window/screen that you want to record…](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-record-screen-and-add-it-to-comments/RS3FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="To complete your recording, click the Stop sharing button to stop the current recording">
    <Frame>
    ![To complete your recording, click the Stop sharing button to stop the current recording](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-record-screen-and-add-it-to-comments/RS4FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="You can add a comment or note in the Comment field…">
    You can add a comment or note in the **Comment** field and click the **Post** icon to save the screen-recorded comment.

    <Frame>
    ![You can add a comment or note in the Comment field…](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-record-screen-and-add-it-to-comments/RS5FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
