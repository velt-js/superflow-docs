title: "How to Sign In to the Superflow Toolbar"
description: "Sign in from the toolbar to comment under your own name and see your team's threads."
icon: "right-to-bracket"
---

Signing in to the Superflow Toolbar lets you comment under your own identity, see and reply to your team's comment threads, and get notified about replies.

<Steps>
  <Step title="On the site under review, find the toolbar and click Sign in">
    The toolbar sits in the bottom corner of the page. The **Sign in** button is on its right side.

    <Frame>
    ![The Superflow toolbar with the Sign in button on the right](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-sign-in-to-the-superflow-toolbar/01-toolbar-signin.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a sign-in method on the Superflow login page">
    You can continue with **Google**, **Microsoft**, or your **work email**. Email sign-in sends you a 6-digit verification code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You are returned to the page, signed in">
    Your avatar appears in the toolbar, and your comments are now attributed to you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
If the project has [guest mode](/how-to-guides/how-to-use-guest-mode) enabled, you can also comment without an account: just add your name when prompted, or stay anonymous.
</Note>
