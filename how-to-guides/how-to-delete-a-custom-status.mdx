## How to Delete a Custom Status

In this article, you will learn how to delete a custom status. When you delete a status, you choose another status to reassign its comments to, so no comments are lost.

1. Go to [Advanced Features](https://app.usesuperflow.com/settings/advanced-features) in your Superflow portal settings.

2. Scroll to the custom status section.

3. Click the status you want to delete. The **Reassign Status** dialog will appear.

<Frame>
![](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-delete-a-custom-status/Delete.png)
</Frame>

4. Select the status that the comments from all projects should be reassigned to once the status is deleted.

5. Click **Delete Status** to confirm.

<Note>
Default statuses cannot be deleted.
</Note>
