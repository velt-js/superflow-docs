---
title: "How to Switch to Mobile View"
description: "Open the page you are reviewing on your phone by scanning a QR code."
icon: "mobile"
---

Reviewing how a page looks on a phone is easiest on an actual phone. The toolbar generates a QR code that opens the same page, in review mode, on your mobile device.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the Superflow Toolbar and click the three-dot menu at its right end">
    <Frame>
    ![The toolbar's menu open, with the Open in Mobile option](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-switch-to-mobile-view/01-more-menu.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Open in Mobile">
    A QR code appears on the screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the QR code with your phone's camera">
    Tap the link that appears. The page opens on your phone with the toolbar active, so you can keep commenting from there. Comments you leave on mobile are tagged with the device, so your developer knows which viewport the feedback is about.
  </Step>
</Steps>
