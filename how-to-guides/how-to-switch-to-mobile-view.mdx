In this article, you will learn how to switch to mobile view. This option enables you to access a page you are working on from your mobile device by accessing the generated QR Code.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the Superflow Toolbar and click the More Options icon">
    <Frame>
    ![Find the Superflow Toolbar and click the More Options icon](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-switch-to-mobile-view/SM1FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="A sub-menu will expand, click the Open in Mobile to select">
  </Step>
  <Step title="A QR Code will appear on the screen">
    On your mobile device, open the **Camera** app and scan the **QR Code**.

    <Frame>
    ![A QR Code will appear on the screen](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-switch-to-mobile-view/SM2.1FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="A Link will appear, select the link…">
    A **Link** will appear, select the link and the mobile version of the current screen of your desktop/laptop will appear on your mobile device.

    <Frame>
    ![A Link will appear, select the link…](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-switch-to-mobile-view/SM3FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
