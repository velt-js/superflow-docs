---
title: "How to Expand the Superflow Toolbar"
description: "Collapse the toolbar out of the way, and expand it again when you need it."
icon: "up-right-and-down-left-from-center"
---

Sometimes the toolbar covers something you need to see. You can collapse it to a small pill without exiting review mode, and expand it again with one click.

To collapse the toolbar, click the **chevron** at its left end. Only the logo stays visible.

<Frame>
![The collapsed Superflow toolbar, showing just the logo and an expand chevron](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-expand-the-superflow-toolbar/01-collapsed.png)
</Frame>

To expand it again, click the **chevron** next to the logo. All the tools come back.

<Frame>
![The expanded Superflow toolbar with all tools visible](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-expand-the-superflow-toolbar/02-expanded.png)
</Frame>

<Tip>
You can also drag the toolbar to a different spot on the page if it is in the way.
</Tip>
