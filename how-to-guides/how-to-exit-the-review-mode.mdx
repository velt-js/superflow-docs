---
title: "How to Exit the Review Mode"
description: "Turn off the toolbar from its menu when you are done reviewing."
icon: "eye-slash"
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the Superflow Toolbar and click the three-dot menu at its right end">
    <Frame>
    ![The toolbar's menu open, with Exit Review Mode at the bottom](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-exit-review-the-mode/01-more-menu.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Exit Review Mode">
    The page reloads without the toolbar. To come back later, [enter review mode](/how-to-guides/how-to-enter-the-review-mode) again by adding `?review=true` to the URL.
  </Step>
</Steps>
