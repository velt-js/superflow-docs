In this article, you will learn how to use the Search Bar to narrow down your comment search.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the Superflow Toolbar and click the Open Comments icon">
    <Frame>
    ![Find the Superflow Toolbar and click the Open Comments icon](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-search-comments/OCS1FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="The Comments Sidebar opens">
    On the top of the **Comments Sidebar**, find and click the **Search comments** field.

    <Frame>
    ![The Comments Sidebar opens](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-search-comments/SC1FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="In the Search comments field, enter the comment keyword">
    <Frame>
    ![In the Search comments field, enter the comment keyword](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-search-comments/SC2FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="When you start typing the keywords…">
    When you start typing the keywords, the **Comments Sidebar** automatically displays related search results.
  </Step>
</Steps>
