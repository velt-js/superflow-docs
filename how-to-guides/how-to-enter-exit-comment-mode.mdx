---
title: "How to Enter/Exit Comment Mode"
description: "Enter comment mode to leave feedback anywhere on the page, and exit with Esc."
icon: "comment-dots"
---

Comment mode is how you leave feedback on the page. While it is active, the page is outlined and a **Comment Mode** banner appears at the top of the window.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the Superflow Toolbar and click the Comment icon">
    You can also press **C** on your keyboard.

    <Frame>
    ![The page in comment mode, with the Comment Mode banner and outline](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-enter-exit-comment-mode/01-comment-mode.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave your feedback">
    In comment mode you can:
    * [Comment on an element](/how-to-guides/how-to-add-a-comment-on-an-element)
    * [Comment on selected text](/how-to-guides/how-to-add-a-comment-to-a-text)
    * [Draw a box anywhere on the page](/how-to-guides/how-to-draw-a-box-comment-on-the-page)
  </Step>
  <Step title="To exit, press Esc">
    You can also click **esc to exit** in the banner at the top of the window.
  </Step>
</Steps>
