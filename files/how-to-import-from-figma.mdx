Import Figma frames into Superflow and review them with the same comments, statuses and share links you use everywhere else. Clients see the design in their browser, with no Figma account or seat required.

## Import your first frames

<Steps>
  <Step title="On the Home page, click Create Project and choose Figma">
    The **Figma** chip sits under the URL field alongside Videos, Images, Lottie and PDF.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your Figma account">
    The first time, Superflow asks you to authorize with Figma. This is a one-time step; you can manage the connection later under **Settings**, then **Integrations**, then **Figma**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the file and select frames">
    Choose which frames you want reviewed and start the import. Superflow shows progress while it pulls the frames in, then opens the design in the file viewer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the review link">
    Click **Share** to invite teammates or send clients the review link. Commenting works exactly like it does on files you upload directly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Keeping the design up to date

When the design changes in Figma, open the Figma menu in the file viewer and choose **Pull Latest & Make New Version**. The updated design comes in as a new version, and earlier feedback stays attached to the earlier version. **Open in Figma** jumps you back to the source file.

<Tip>
Combine this with [version approvals](/files/how-to-approve-a-file-version): pull the latest, review it, then approve the version the client signed off on.
</Tip>
