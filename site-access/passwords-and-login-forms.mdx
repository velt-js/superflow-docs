Three of the five Site Access options need almost no setup. Pick the one that matches what you see when you open your site in a private browser window.

## Option 3: Shared password

For a site with a single password and no username, which is what Webflow, Shopify, Squarespace and Framer produce.

Choose **Single password** and paste the password. That is the whole setup.

## Option 4: Browser sign in

For a site that shows a browser popup asking for a username and password before the page draws.

Choose **Username and password (HTTP auth)** and enter the shared pair.

## Option 5: Login form

For a login form on your own site.

Choose **Username and password**, provide an account, and then **check the result**. Every login form is different, so this option is best effort: Superflow looks for conventional username and password fields and a submit button.

<Warning>
If your form is unusual, use **Test access** to confirm it works before relying on it.
</Warning>
