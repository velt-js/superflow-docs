---
title: "How to Remove Commenters"
description: "Remove a Guest from your workspace."
icon: "user-xmark"
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="On the Superflow Dashboard, click Users in the left sidebar and open the Guests tab">
    Each guest row shows how many projects they are involved in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the person and click the trash icon on their row">
    The removal takes effect immediately: they can no longer comment on the projects they were invited to, and their guest seat is freed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Removing a guest does not delete the comments they already left. Those stay on the project with their name attached.
</Note>
