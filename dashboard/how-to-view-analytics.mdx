The **Analytics** page in the left sidebar shows how review work is flowing across your workspace: how much feedback is coming in, how fast it gets resolved, and where the bottlenecks are.

<Frame>
![The Analytics Overview tab with comment stats and resolution times](/images/dashboard/how-to-view-analytics/01-overview.png)
</Frame>

<Note>
Analytics is currently in **Beta**. Use the lookback period dropdown in the header to change the date range for every tab except Past Data.
</Note>

## The four tabs

<Steps>
  <Step title="Overview shows the whole team">
    A status trend chart over time, plus two stat cards: **Comment Stats** (comments added, comments resolved, median response time) and **Resolution Time** (average, median, fastest and slowest). **Top Projects by Tasks** ranks projects by open comments, with a link straight to each project's board.
  </Step>
  <Step title="For me shows your own queue">
    **Top Projects by Pending Tasks** ranks the projects where you have open work, and **Awaiting your response** lists the comments waiting on you specifically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="People shows who is waiting on whom">
    **Pending Tasks** per person shows where feedback is piling up, and **Fastest Resolution Time** shows who resolves feedback quickest.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Past Data holds your history">
    Data from before March 2026 lives on its own tab with comments and replies, median response time, top active projects, and active users for that period.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Use **Top Projects by Tasks** at the start of the week to decide where review effort goes. It is the fastest way to spot a project quietly accumulating unresolved feedback.
</Tip>
