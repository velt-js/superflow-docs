---
title: "How to Remove Admin Users"
description: "Remove a Team User from your workspace."
icon: "user-minus"
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="On the Superflow Dashboard, click Users in the left sidebar">
    The **Team Users** tab lists everyone with admin access.

    <Frame>
    ![The Users page's Team Users tab with a remove icon on each row](/images/product-features/user-roles-explained/01-users-page.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the person and click the trash icon on their row">
    The removal takes effect immediately: they lose access to the workspace and their seat is freed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
You cannot remove yourself or the workspace owner. To take back an invitation that has not been accepted yet, use the **Invited** tab instead.
</Note>
