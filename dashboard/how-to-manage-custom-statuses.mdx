---
title: "How to Use Custom Comment Statuses"
description: "Replace the default Open, In Progress and Resolved workflow with statuses that match how your team actually works."
icon: "tags"
---

Out of the box, comments move through the default statuses. If your team's workflow has more stages, for example **Awaiting Client**, **In Development**, or **Ready for QA**, you can add your own custom statuses. They appear everywhere statuses do: on the comment on the page, in the sidebar, and as columns on the Kanban board.

## Turn on custom statuses

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings, then Advanced Features">
    Under **Comment Settings**, turn on the **Custom comment statuses** toggle. A status editor appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your statuses">
    Add each status with a name and a color, and arrange them in the order your workflow moves through them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Custom statuses are available on paid plans. While custom statuses exist, the feature cannot be switched off; delete the custom statuses first.
</Note>

## Add a status directly from the board

On any project's Kanban board, hover between two columns and click the **+** zone to create a new status right where you want the column. You can also drag columns to reorder them.

## Deleting a status

When you delete a custom status that still has comments in it, Superflow asks you where to move those comments, so nothing gets lost.

<Tip>
Keep the list short. Five or six columns is the sweet spot; beyond that, cards stop moving and the board stops telling you anything.
</Tip>
