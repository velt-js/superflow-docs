---
title: "How to Set Up Email Summaries"
description: "Get a digest of comment activity across your projects, on the schedule you choose."
icon: "envelope"
---

Email summaries send you a digest of what happened across your projects, so you can stay on top of feedback without living in the dashboard.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings, then Advanced Features">
    Scroll to the **Email Notifications** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a frequency for email summaries">
    Choose how often the digest arrives from the frequency dropdown, or pick **None** to turn summaries off. Until you change it, Superflow uses the default schedule.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send yourself a test email">
    Use **Send test email** to check what the digest looks like and confirm it lands in your inbox rather than a spam folder.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Superflow also sends real-time notifications through the [Slack integration](/Integrations/how-to-integrate-with-slack) and [webhooks](/Integrations/how-to-use-webhooks). Email summaries are the low-noise option: one digest, on your schedule.
</Tip>
