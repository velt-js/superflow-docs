---
title: "How to Invite Admin Users"
description: "Invite a Team User, who gets admin access to every project in your workspace."
icon: "user-shield"
---

Admins are called **Team Users** in Superflow. They can access every project, create projects, and manage the workspace. See [Team Users and Guests](/product-features/user-roles-explained).

<Steps>
  <Step title="On the Superflow Dashboard, click Users in the left sidebar">
    Then click **Invite Users** in the top right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the person's email and keep the user type as Team User">
    <Frame>
    ![The Invite Users dialog with Team User selected](/images/dashboard/how-to-invite-users-to-your-project/01-invite-modal.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite">
    They receive an invitation email. Until they accept, the invitation is listed on the **Invited** tab, where you can withdraw it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Team Users consume user seats from your plan. If seats are full, the dialog shows your usage and offers an upgrade, or you can withdraw a pending invitation to free a seat. See [What is a Seat](/billing/what-is-a-seat).
</Note>
