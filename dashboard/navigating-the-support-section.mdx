In this article, you will learn how to navigate to the Support section, access useful help topics, and/or reach out to one of our support agents.

Access the Superflow Dashboard by going to [app.usesuperflow.com](app.usesuperflow.com). Alternatively, when you are in Review Mode, you can use the method below:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the Superflow Toolbar and click the More Options icon">
    <Frame>
    ![Find the Superflow Toolbar and click the More Options icon](/images/dashboard/how-to-access-project-settings/how-to-access-project-settings/INT1FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="A sub-menu will expand, click the View Project option to select">
  </Step>
  <Step title="A new tab will direct you to the Project Dashboard page">
    <Note>
    By default, you will be directed to the Dashboard for Comment Status screen when accessing the **Project Dashboard**.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find and click the Contact Support icon on the bottom right of the screen">
    <Note>
    alternatively, you can click the **Contact Support** module.
    </Note>

    <Frame>
    ![Find and click the Contact Support icon on the bottom right of the screen](/images/dashboard/navigating-the-support-section/SUP1FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="The Support window will expand">
    In this window you can do the following:

    - **Messages** - Send us a message using this option.
    - **Help** - Access the Help option where you can search for help articles.
    - **Send us a Message**
    - **Docs** - Directs you to the How-To Guides and FAQs page.
    - **Install Videos** -  Directs you to the Superflow YouTube channel where you can find the Superflow Install Guides.
    - **Book Demo** - This directs you to Superflow’s Calendly scheduler app where you can book for a demo.

    <Frame>
    ![The Support window will expand](/images/dashboard/navigating-the-support-section/SUP2FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
