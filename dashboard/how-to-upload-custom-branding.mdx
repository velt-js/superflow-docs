---
title: "How to Upload Custom Branding"
description: "Put your agency's logo on the toolbar and the dashboard, so clients see your brand."
icon: "palette"
---

With custom branding, the review toolbar your clients see and the dashboard your team uses both carry your logo instead of Superflow's.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings, then Custom Branding">
    On the Superflow Dashboard, click **Settings** in the left sidebar and open the **Custom Branding** tab.

    <Frame>
    ![The Custom Branding tab with the Toolbar and Admin Portal upload targets](/images/dashboard/how-to-upload-custom-branding/01-custom-branding.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your Toolbar logo">
    Click the edit button on the **Toolbar** preview and pick a **square** logomark, SVG or PNG, up to 2MB. It replaces the Superflow mark on the toolbar your reviewers see.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your Admin Portal logo">
    Click the edit button on the **Admin Portal** preview and pick a wide **4:1** wordmark, SVG or PNG, up to 2MB. It replaces the Superflow logo in the dashboard sidebar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Both previews update live, and each logo can be removed to go back to the default.

<Note>
Custom branding is available on paid plans. If the tab shows an upgrade prompt, your current plan does not include it. See [which parts of the site can be custom branded](/product-features/which-parts-of-the-site-would-I-be-able-to-custom-brand).
</Note>
