---
title: "How to Invite Users to your Project"
description: "Invite your team and your clients: Team Users get the whole workspace, Guests get just their project."
icon: "user-plus"
---

There are two user types in Superflow. **Team Users** (Admins) can access every project and manage the workspace. **Guests** (commenters) can only comment on the projects they are invited to. See [Team Users and Guests](/product-features/user-roles-explained) for the full comparison.

<Steps>
  <Step title="On the Superflow Dashboard, click Users in the left sidebar">
    Then click the **Invite Users** button in the top right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter one or more email addresses">
    Type or paste emails, separated by commas. Each becomes a removable pill.

    <Frame>
    ![The Invite Users dialog with the email field and user type dropdown](/images/dashboard/how-to-invite-users-to-your-project/01-invite-modal.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the user type">
    - **Team User**: joins the whole workspace with access to every project.
    - **Guest**: tied to a single project. Pick the project when prompted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite">
    Each address receives an invitation email. Pending invitations appear on the **Invited** tab, where you can withdraw them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Clients often do not need an invitation at all. With [guest mode](/how-to-guides/how-to-use-guest-mode) enabled, anyone with the review link can comment. You can also click **Share** on any project to copy the review link directly.
</Tip>
