---
title: "How to Invite Commenters"
description: "Invite a Guest, who can comment on a single project without seeing the rest of your workspace."
icon: "user-pen"
---

Commenters are called **Guests** in Superflow. They can leave and reply to comments on the projects they are invited to, and nothing else. This is the right role for clients and outside reviewers. See [Team Users and Guests](/product-features/user-roles-explained).

<Steps>
  <Step title="On the Superflow Dashboard, click Users in the left sidebar">
    Then click **Invite Users** in the top right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the person's email and switch the user type to Guest">
    Pick which project they should have access to.

    <Frame>
    ![The Invite Users dialog, where the user type dropdown offers Guest](/images/dashboard/how-to-invite-users-to-your-project/01-invite-modal.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite">
    They receive an email that takes them straight to the project in review mode.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
If the project has [guest mode](/how-to-guides/how-to-use-guest-mode) enabled, you can skip the invitation entirely: copy the review link and anyone who opens it can comment.
</Tip>
