---
title: "How to View All Comments on a Project"
description: "See every comment on a project in one place, from the project board."
icon: "comments"
---

To see every comment on a project, open the project from the Home page on the Superflow Dashboard. The **Comments** tab shows all feedback as cards, grouped into status columns, with a count in the tab label.

<Frame>
![The project board showing all comments grouped by status](/images/dashboard/how-to-view-all-comments-on-a-project/01-board.png)
</Frame>

- The **Comments** count on the Home page's project list gives you the same total at a glance, including draft comments.
- Use the search box and filters to narrow by person or page, and click any card to read the full thread and reply.
- The **Agent Comments** tab next to it collects findings from [AI agents](/agents/overview) separately.

<Tip>
Want the comments outside Superflow? [Export them as JSON or CSV](/how-to-guides/how-can-I-export-comments), or connect [Slack, Asana, ClickUp or Monday](/Integrations/how-to-integrate-with-slack) to push them into your tracker as they happen.
</Tip>
