## What are Custom Statuses?

Custom statuses are a Superflow feature that lets you create personalized comment statuses which fit your team's internal workflow. When adding a comment, you can choose a custom status by clicking on the comment header, and those comments will show up in the Kanban board within the Superflow portal.

This is especially useful for agencies that have their own review stages built to support their internal QA process, typically managed in a task management tool, and want to bring those stages into their website review workflow.
