---
title: "What is a Seat?"
description: "Seats are how Superflow counts the people in your workspace: user seats for your team, guest seats for your clients."
icon: "chair"
---

Superflow plans include two kinds of seats:

- **User seats** are for Team Users (Admins): the people on your own team who can access every project, create projects, and manage the workspace.
- **Guest seats** are for Guests (commenters): clients and outside reviewers who are invited to specific projects.

Your current seat usage is shown on the **Billing** page under **Settings**, and you can add seats there when the team grows.

<Note>
Anonymous visitors who comment through [guest mode](/product-features/what-is-guest-mode) with just a name do not consume a seat. Seats count invited, signed-in users.
</Note>

See [Team Users and Guests](/product-features/user-roles-explained) for a full comparison of the two roles.
