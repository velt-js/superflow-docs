An agent is a reusable check that you describe in plain English. Superflow runs it against your projects and reports back what it finds. There is no manual QA pass and no checklist to work through by hand.

</Frame>

<Note>
  Agents are currently in **Beta**. Each run consumes credits from your workspace balance, and the exact cost is shown before you confirm a run.
</Note>

## Turning an existing checklist into agents

If your team already has a QA checklist, you do not have to rewrite it. Superflow reads the file, works out which tasks can be verified on a rendered page, and tells you which ones cannot. Settings inside a CMS, for example, are flagged as excluded rather than silently dropped.

## Running one against a project

Pick the project and the path, choose desktop or mobile, and Superflow shows the exact cost against your balance before you confirm. Findings come back as the issue, a suggested fix, and the markup it sits in. Every run is kept, so you can compare a new one against what the agent found last time.

## Running them from your issue tracker

Everything above is a run you start yourself. An **agent workflow** starts the run for you: a ticket moving into QA dispatches the agents, the findings land back on that ticket as a comment, and the card moves to your fix column — or to Done when every agent comes back clean.

## Sites behind a login

An agent can only report on a page it can actually read. If your site sits behind a password, a login, or single sign on, configure **Site Access** in your project settings first — otherwise the run stops and tells you, rather than describing the sign in screen as though it were your site.

<Card title="Letting Superflow read your protected site" icon="lock" href="/site-access/overview" horizontal>
  Work out which of the five options you need, then set it up.
</Card>

## The agent Superflow builds for you

You do not have to write the first agent. Superflow reads the feedback your team and your clients already leave on projects, turns each recurring note into a check, and puts the result at the top of your agent list as your **Workspace Agent**. Add your brand guidelines to its knowledge base and the same checks apply to every project you point it at.

## Asking across every project at once

Agents check one page at a time. **Ask AI** works the other way round: ask one plain-English question and Superflow answers from the review threads across every project — the recurring issues, which projects they sit in, and what to do about them. Any answer can be turned into a report, either immediately or on a weekly schedule that emails itself to your team.
