In this article, you will learn how to use the AI Co-Pilot feature. It is important to note that you have to highlight more than two (2) words to activate the AI Toolbar.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ensure that the Review Mode is enabled and the Superflow Toolbar is active">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Highlight the text that you want to use the AI Co-Pilot on">
    <Frame>
    ![Highlight the text that you want to use the AI Co-Pilot on](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-use-the-ai-co-pilot/ACP1FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="An AI Toolbar will appear, click Rewrite">
  </Step>
  <Step title="The Rewrite dialog box will appear with AI-generated suggestions">
    To generate more suggestions, click the **Refresh** icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="To select a rewritten text, click its Radio Button">
    <Frame>
    ![To select a rewritten text, click its Radio Button](/images/how-to-guides/how-to-use-the-ai-co-pilot/ACP2FINAL.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="After selecting the preferred rewritten text, click the Create Task button">
  </Step>
</Steps>
