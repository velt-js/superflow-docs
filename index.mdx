## Start with agents

Agents are reusable checks you describe in plain English. Superflow runs them against your projects and reports what it finds: spelling, broken links, accessibility, SEO, or anything else you can describe.

<Note>
  Agents are currently in **Beta**. Each run consumes credits from your workspace balance, and the cost is always shown before you confirm.
</Note>
