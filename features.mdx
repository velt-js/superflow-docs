Superflow is the fastest way for agencies and web teams to review, QA and approve websites and creative assets. This page is the map: what each feature does, who it is for, and where to learn more.

## Review live websites

Comment directly on the real site, not on a screenshot of it. Superflow installs as a single script, and the toolbar appears when you add `?review=true` to any URL.

<Frame caption="The Superflow toolbar, docked in the corner of the site under review">
![The Superflow toolbar with comment, huddle, sidebar, share and sign in controls](/images/features/toolbar-closeup.png)
</Frame>

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Comment on anything" icon="comment-dots" href="/how-to-guides/how-to-add-a-comment-on-an-element">
    Pin a comment to an element, highlight text, or draw a box anywhere on the page. Every comment captures the page, device and screen size automatically.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Record instead of type" icon="microphone" href="/how-to-guides/how-to-record-voice-notes">
    Voice notes, screen recordings and video comments, attached right where the feedback belongs.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Statuses and priorities" icon="list-check" href="/how-to-guides/how-to-change-status">
    Every comment is a task. Set status and priority on the page, then track it on the Kanban board.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Comment screenshots" icon="camera" href="/product-features/comment-screenshots">
    Superflow captures a screenshot with each comment, so feedback still makes sense after the page changes.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## AI review agents

Agents are reusable checks you describe in plain English. Superflow runs them against your projects and reports findings: spelling, broken links, accessibility, brand consistency, or anything else you can describe.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Create an agent" icon="wand-magic-sparkles" href="/agents/how-to-create-a-new-agent">
    Describe the check in plain English. Superflow builds and stress-tests the agent for you.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Turn a checklist into agents" icon="file-import" href="/agents/how-to-create-agents-from-a-checklist">
    Upload the QA checklist you already use as a PDF or CSV, and Superflow extracts one agent per check.
  </Card>
  <Card title="The Workspace Agent" icon="clipboard-check" href="/agents/workspace-agent">
    One built-in agent that enforces a living checklist of rules your whole team maintains.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Review agent findings" icon="triangle-exclamation" href="/agents/how-to-review-agent-findings">
    Findings appear as pins on the page and as a dedicated tab on the project board.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Agent runs consume [AI credits](/billing/ai-credits) from your workspace balance. The cost is always shown before you confirm a run.

## Client review without friction

Your clients should never need a login, a plugin, or a tutorial to leave feedback.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Guest mode" icon="user-group" href="/how-to-guides/how-to-use-guest-mode">
    Clients comment without creating an account. Optionally let them sign in, or stay anonymous with just a name.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Review links" icon="link" href="/dashboard/how-create-a-new-project">
    Share a single link that opens the site with the toolbar ready. Password protection available for sensitive work.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Files, PDFs and media

Websites are half the job. Superflow also reviews the assets around them: PDFs, images, videos, Lottie animations, and Figma files.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Upload a file" icon="file-arrow-up" href="/files/how-to-upload-a-file">
    Drop in a PDF, image, video or Lottie file and get a review link in seconds.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Import from Figma" icon="figma" href="/files/how-to-import-from-figma">
    Connect Figma, pick frames, and review designs with the same comments and statuses as everything else.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Versions and approvals" icon="circle-check" href="/files/how-to-approve-a-file-version">
    Every file keeps a version history. Approve the final version so everyone knows what shipped.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Comment on a file" icon="comment" href="/files/how-to-comment-on-a-file">
    The same commenting tools you use on live sites, on any uploaded asset.

</CardGroup>

## The dashboard

The Superflow dashboard at [app.usesuperflow.com](https://app.usesuperflow.com) is where the work gets tracked and the workspace gets managed.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Kanban board" icon="table-columns" href="/dashboard/how-to-use-the-kanban-board">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Analytics" icon="chart-line" href="/dashboard/how-to-view-analytics">
    Comment volume, resolution times, busiest projects, and who is waiting on whom.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Team and guests" icon="users" href="/product-features/user-roles-explained">

  <Card title="Custom branding" icon="palette" href="/dashboard/how-to-upload-custom-branding">
    Put your agency's logo on the toolbar and the dashboard, so clients see your brand, not ours.

    Download every comment on a project as JSON or CSV.

  </Card>
</CardGroup>
