<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter Your Site URL">
    Enter the site URL you want Superflow to be installed in.

    <Frame>
    ![Wix — Enter Your Site URL](/images/enter-url-final.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Wix from the list of Platforms">
    Choose Wix from the list of options to install Superflow with Wix.

    <Frame>
    ![Wix — Select Wix from the list of Platforms](/images/select-wix-final.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy your unique Superflow code">
    You can copy your unique Superflow code from the onboarding flow or from your project settings in your dashboard.

    <Frame>
    ![Wix — Copy your unique Superflow code](/images/copy-code-final.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Settings on your Wix Site and then click Custom Code">
    You can get to Settings at the bottom left of the side menu. Then scroll down and click Custom Code.

    <Frame>
    ![Wix — Go to Settings on your Wix Site and then click Custom Code](/images/code-wix-final.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your unique Superflow code in the Body - End section">
    Scroll down and add your unique Superflow code in the Body - End section. Be sure to save.

    <Frame>
    ![Wix — Add your unique Superflow code in the Body - End section](/images/footer-wix-final.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish your Wix site">
    To publish your Wix site first go to your home page and then click Edit Site. 

    Next, click the publish button in the top right to publish your Wix site.

    <Frame>
    ![Wix — Publish your Wix site](/images/publish-wix-final.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your Installation">
    Verify your Superflow installation by clicking the Verify button on the onboarding flow.

    <Frame>
    ![Wix — Verify your Installation](/images/verify-final.png)
    </Frame>

    If successful, you should be redirected to your published Wix site and see the following success message:

    <Frame>
    ![Wix — Verify your Installation](/images/verified-final.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Troubleshooting">
    Having trouble getting your verifying your installation? Here are the common mistakes users encounter when installing Superflow:

    - Did you forget to save the Custom Code?
    - Did you forget to publish your site?
    - Is the browser still loading a cached version?
    - Did you add `?review=true` at the end of your url?
    - Did you publish your changes on a staging URL while you are trying to access a Prod URL?
    - Did you accidentally install multiple Superflow scripts in the Custom Code?
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to your Wix site">
    Now that you have verified that Superflow has been installed on your Wix site, navigate to your published site and add `?review=true` at the end of the URL to enable Superflow. 

    `Example: mywebsite.wix.com/?review=true`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test out Superflow">
    You should now see a Superflow toolbar on the bottom right of you screen.

    <Frame>
    ![Wix — Test out Superflow](/images/toolbar-final.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dropping your first comment">
    Try leaving a comment on your page.

    You can leave a comment in several different ways:
    - By highlighting any text
    - By hovering over any element on the page
    - By drawing a box anywhere on the page

    You can also leave voice and screen recordings in your comments as well!

    <Frame>
    ![Wix — Dropping your first comment](/images/demo.gif)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
