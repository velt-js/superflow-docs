<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter Your Site URL">
    Enter the site URL you want Superflow to be installed in.

    <Frame>
    ![Elementor: Enter Your Site URL](/images/enter-url-final.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Other from the list of Platforms">
    Choose Other from the list of options to install Superflow with Elementor.

    <Frame>
    ![Elementor: Select Other from the list of Platforms](/images/select-other-final.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy your unique Superflow code">
    You can copy your unique Superflow code from the onboarding flow or from your project settings in your dashboard.

    <Frame>
    ![Elementor: Copy your unique Superflow code](/images/copy-code-final.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to your Wordpress Dashboard, then click on Custom Code under Elementor">
    Go to your Wordpress Dashboard then click on Elementor in the side navigation. 

    Next, click on Custom Code and then click Create New Custom Code to add your unique Superflow code

    <Frame>
    ![Elementor: Go to your Wordpress Dashboard, then click on Custom Code under Elementor](/images/settings-elementor-final.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Your unique Superflow Code">
    Create a new Custom Code title and paste in your unique Superflow Code

    <Frame>
    ![Elementor: Add Your unique Superflow Code](/images/code-elementor-add-final.png)
    </Frame>

    Be sure to change the location to `<Body> - End`.

    <Frame>
    ![Elementor: Add Your unique Superflow Code](/images/elementor-body-final.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish your Elementor site">
    Click the Publish button at the right of the page to publish your Elementor site.

    <Frame>
    ![Elementor: Publish your Elementor site](/images/publish-elementor-final.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your Installation">
    Verify your Superflow installation by clicking the Verify button on the onboarding flow.

    <Frame>
    ![Elementor: Verify your Installation](/images/verify-final.png)
    </Frame>

    If successful, you should be redirected to your published Elementor site and see the following success message:

    <Frame>
    ![Elementor: Verify your Installation](/images/verified-final.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Troubleshooting">
    Having trouble getting your verifying your installation? Here are the common mistakes users encounter when installing Superflow:

    - Did you forget to save the Custom Code?
    - Did you forget to publish your site?
    - Is the browser still loading a cached version?
    - Did you add `?review=true` at the end of your url?
    - Did you publish your changes on a staging URL while you are trying to access a Prod URL?
    - Did you accidentally install multiple Superflow scripts in the Custom Code?
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to your Elementor site">
    Now that you have verified that Superflow has been installed on your Elementor site, navigate to your published site and add `?review=true` at the end of the URL to enable Superflow. 

    `Example: mywebsite.elementor.com/?review=true`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test out Superflow">
    You should now see a Superflow toolbar on the bottom right of you screen.

    <Frame>
    ![Elementor: Test out Superflow](/images/toolbar-final.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dropping your first comment">
    Try leaving a comment on your page.

    You can leave a comment in several different ways:
    - By highlighting any text
    - By hovering over any element on the page
    - By drawing a box anywhere on the page

    You can also leave voice and screen recordings in your comments as well!

    <Frame>
    ![Elementor: Dropping your first comment](/images/demo.gif)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
